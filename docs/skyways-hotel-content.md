## 🗣️ Guest Highlights

### ✅ Positives
- Described as a "no-frills budget clean hotel" — praised for cleanliness
- Friendly and helpful staff, consistently mentioned in reviews
- Excellent value compared to other LAX-area hotels
- Hertz/Thrifty rental car offices directly across the street — free shuttle 24/7
- One block away from new public transit interchange station
- Private enclosed courtyard — quiet and pleasant with plants, tables, and chairs
- Comfortable, spacious rooms relative to price
- Accepts cash + cash deposit

### ⚠️ Caveats
- Under flight path — plane noise can be disruptive (earplugs recommended), though flights stop late evening
- No parking on-site
- Limited nearby amenities after 9 PM
- Wi-Fi can be inconsistent per some reviews
- Construction noise possible due to LAX Automated People Mover project nearby

---

## 📋 Summary

Skyways Hotel is a budget-friendly, no-frills 2-star airport hotel ideally situated less than 1 mile from LAX. It is best suited for short layovers, early departures, or late arrivals. Its standout features are the on-site Aliki's Greek Taverna (highly rated), clean and spacious rooms, helpful multilingual staff, and pricing well below the LAX-area average. Guests sensitive to aircraft noise should request a courtyard-facing room.
